# Nagi 1.1.7

VersionCode 16. Google Play is the sole official distribution channel.
Release preparation is in progress; upload, review submission and public availability are separate states.

- Ask compatible keyboards not to learn from private input, covering addresses, app editors and website fields.
- Keep private tabs when returning Home, switching apps or leaving and reopening the private screen.
- Clear the private workspace and isolated site data when ending private browsing or removing the app task from Recents, including when Android has stopped the background observer service.
- Keep normal browsing data separate and preserve existing IME actions and CJK composition.

Private tabs remain memory-only. Android process termination, force-stop or reboot can end the session before task removal. Keyboard privacy indicators and honoring the no-learning request depend on the installed keyboard.

Verification on 2026-11-05: 175 Play Debug unit tests, Debug/Release lint, Debug/test APK builds, and 22 selected API-36 instrumentation tests passed. The device cases cover private task lifetime, isolated storage cleanup, Compose/WebView keyboard metadata, Reader, and CJK composition. Physical-device and process-death restoration checks were not performed. Signed artifact checks and Play state are recorded separately.

See the [localized Play notes](../play-store/release-notes-1.1.7.txt). Source tags and release notes do not establish Google Play availability.

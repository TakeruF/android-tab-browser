# Nagi 1.1.2

Release candidate, versionCode 11. The user supplied and approved the four-language Play release notes on 2026-11-01. Play submission and rollout are authorized; store status is tracked separately.

- Automatically pop out a user-started video when switching to another tab or Space. Returning to its source tab restores playback inside the page. Autoplaying page demos stay in their page.
- Continue fullscreen or existing pop-out video in Android picture-in-picture when going Home. Ordinary page videos, including autoplay, do not trigger PiP.
- Independently enable or disable automatic tab pop-out and Home/PiP in Settings; existing preferences are preserved.
- Remove the manual pop-out button from websites and fullscreen controls.
- Keep in-app video windows free of controls until tapped. Overlay Lucide controls and playback time on the video, then hide them after inactivity; drag to move, pinch or drag a corner with the mouse to resize. Controls keep localized accessibility labels and tooltips. Home-screen PiP keeps the system's size limits; the app does not request a minimum size.
- Restore previews for unstarted YouTube channel videos while a playlist miniplayer is playing.
- Provide a dedicated monochrome launcher layer for system-themed icons and a neutral charcoal gradient (#353535 to #111111) in system dark mode; this palette follows the supplied reference and is not an official OEM or launcher-supplied color.
- Keep password autofill unavailable until approval is granted: disable native filling and hide the manual action and entire Settings section, without displaying an awaiting-approval notice. The integration remains available only to explicit test fixtures.
- Pass the app’s effective light/dark theme to websites through native `prefers-color-scheme`, including existing and newly opened tabs. Preserve the white default canvas of light websites such as Asahi instead of changing transparent backgrounds to black.
- Preserve the page zoom when returning from a video pop-out to its source tab.
- Keep the full video frame visible when shrinking or enlarging a window, including desktop pages whose visible viewport shifts during resizing.

See the [four-language release notes](../play-store/release-notes-1.1.2.txt). Google Play remains the sole official distribution channel; local debug APKs are for verification.

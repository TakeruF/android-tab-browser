## Changes

- Improve phone browsing with a single-row bottom address bar, New tab, tab count
  and page menu, without a Home button. Keep the existing top-address setting.
- Fit the tab sheet to its content, capped at 85% of the available height; remove
  the redundant selected-tab check while retaining its highlighted row.
- Keep browser footers and the wide sidebar behind the on-screen keyboard;
  resize page editors separately from the Command Bar's input window.
- Request mobile websites by default on phones, including landscape, while
  respecting per-site choices and retaining tablet behavior.
- Flatten phone page edges and remove the idle top divider when the address bar
  is at the bottom, placing content directly below system-bar/cutout safety.
- Replace provider-generated page errors and the red strip with a native,
  centered icon, localized explanation and Retry. Distinguish connection,
  timeout, certificate and other errors; preserve server-authored HTTP responses
  and healthy pages with failed subresources. Certificate validation is retained.
- Cache and select website icons independently for light and dark themes, with
  bounded persistent storage and unchanged live pages during theme changes.

## Limits and verification

Provider behavior, OEM keyboards and physical devices require separate checks.
Retaining a live engine does not guarantee draft restoration after process death.
Website-supplied icons may not provide both theme variants. The error screen
does not override server-authored 404/500 content or bypass certificate checks.
See [page errors](../PAGE_ERRORS.md), [compact UI](../COMPACT_UI.md),
[favicons](../FAVICONS.md), and [release verification](../RELEASE_1.4.0_VERIFICATION.md)
for exact coverage and the recorded distribution stage.
